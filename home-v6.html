    // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    Questions About Your Order? We've Got Answers

    Everything you need to know about prescriptions, delivery, returns and payments. Can't find your answer? Our pharmacists are one call away.

    Still have questions?

    Chat with a licensed pharmacist — free, 24/7.

    Call Now Live Chat
    Do I need a prescription to order medicines?

    Over-the-counter products, vitamins and personal care items can be ordered freely. Prescription-only medicines (marked Rx Required) need a valid prescription, which you can upload at checkout or through our prescription form.

    How fast is delivery and how much does it cost?

    Orders placed before 6 PM are delivered the same day within the city, usually in 2–4 hours. Delivery is free on orders over $49; a flat $3.99 fee applies below that.

    Are your medicines genuine and safely stored?

    Yes. We source directly from licensed manufacturers and authorised distributors. Temperature-sensitive products such as insulin and vaccines are kept and shipped in cold-chain packaging.

    Can I return or exchange a product?

    Unopened, non-prescription products can be returned within 7 days for a full refund. For safety reasons, prescription medicines can only be returned if they were damaged or incorrect.

    Can I speak to a pharmacist before buying?

    Absolutely — our licensed pharmacists are available 24/7 by phone or chat to help with dosage, side effects, drug interactions or choosing the right product, free of charge.

    Do you accept insurance and HSA/FSA cards?

    We accept most major insurance plans for prescription medicines, as well as HSA and FSA cards for eligible products. Add your insurance details in your account to see covered prices.
